What is returned

Raw and temperature-scaled probabilities, 90% and 95% split-conformal sets, GC content, and sigma-model MC-dropout uncertainty.

Sigma-factor output is most meaningful for promoter-like sequences. These research models are dataset-specific and are not wet-lab validated.

The page never substitutes dummy predictions when a backend or output is unavailable.
