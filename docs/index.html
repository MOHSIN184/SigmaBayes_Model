BayesSigma

A benchmark for 81 bp bacterial promoter recognition and six-class sigma-factor classification that evaluates predictions alongside calibration, MC-dropout uncertainty, and split-conformal prediction sets.

Beyond a single accuracy number

Two biological tasks

Binary Non-Promoter vs Promoter classification and six-way assignment across Sigma24, 28, 32, 38, 54, and 70.

Model comparison

One-hot CNNs, explicit k-mer baselines, and fused CNN+k-mer networks are compared on held-out data and five-fold cross-validation.

Reliability layer

Temperature scaling, MC dropout, and split conformal prediction expose confidence quality, uncertainty, and plausible label sets.
